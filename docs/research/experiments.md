# Experiment: 20-commit evidence evaluation

**Goal:** judge whether the evidence this pipeline collects (`repository.json`, the
builder methods that would become `commit.json`, and the four Milestone 5A extractors,
referred to below as `evidence.json` per the user's naming) is *sufficient* for an
experienced engineer or an LLM to understand a commit and reason about its impact —
**not** whether the commit's code is correct.

**Method:** 20 real commits across 4 repositories, deliberately mixed for size/type/repo
maturity, picked by scanning real history (not hand-picked to look good):

- `fastapi/fastapi` (6) — large, mature OSS. Docs bot, CI, release, feature, refactor,
  large automated dependency bump.
- `pallets/flask` (5) — medium, mature OSS. Typo fix, dependency update, release,
  CI/security tooling, multi-file bugfix.
- `Nogrunt-Collaborations-Private-limited/tcx_nogrunt-1` (6) — the user's private company
  repo. Bugfix, two security-flavored escaping fixes, two features, a cleanup/deletion
  commit.
- A local, personal repo found on this device, `~/Projects/Triple` (3) — 5 total commits
  in its whole history, genuinely messy: `.DS_Store` and `.pyc` files committed, vague
  messages ("initial", "additional changes to duplicate-check").

Defaults in effect throughout (all previously flagged as stopgaps, not final designs):
`co_change` bounded to the last 50 historical commits per file, top 10 partners reported;
`local_module_context` capped at 20 siblings; `repository.json`'s `contributors` capped
at 20 (not relevant per-commit, noted for completeness).
